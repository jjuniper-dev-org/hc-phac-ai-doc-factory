Related Jira: EAID-9 / EAID-21  

## Purpose

Define the repeatable enterprise mechanism that connects AI strategy, capabilities, governance, architecture, data, platforms, acquisition, delivery and operations across HC/PHAC.

## Scope

EAIF covers:

- principles, scope and authority
- AI capability/classification and reuse
- demand, intake and inventory
- assessment, prioritization and routing
- governance triage, decision authority and accountability
- data readiness and data architecture
- platform architecture, Pattern Zero and reference patterns
- acquisition, vendor/model assessment and software standards
- delivery and shared responsibility
- production authorization and lifecycle change
- AI-Ops, DataOps, FinOps and observability
- evidence, metadata, auditability and exception handling
- enterprise readiness, maturity and value realization

## Source discipline

Normative statements must be derived from `data/eaif_requirements.csv` and traceable to `data/eaif_evidence.csv`.

Working interpretations, proposals, observations and meeting intelligence must remain visibly classified and must not be silently promoted to approved HC/PHAC policy or architecture.

## Required review gates

- Writing quality review
- Enterprise architecture review
- Data architecture review
- Microsoft technical review where relevant
- TOGAF / architecture method review
- Policy / governance review
- Accessibility / plain-language review
- Citation and evidence review
- Final human editorial review

## Current working assumption

This document is a working framework draft. It is not an approved HC/PHAC policy instrument, enterprise standard or target-state architecture until the relevant elements are reviewed and approved through the appropriate governance path.
