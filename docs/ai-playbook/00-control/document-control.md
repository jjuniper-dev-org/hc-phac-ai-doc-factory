## Purpose

Create an enterprise AI Playbook for governed, auditable, reusable, and responsible AI adoption across HC/PHAC.

## Scope

The playbook covers:

- AI intake and triage
- Use case assessment
- Risk and readiness review
- Platform routing
- Responsible AI controls
- ADM / DM accountability
- Architecture and governance gates
- PATH / HAIL / Purview alignment
- AI operations and monitoring
- Reusable AI patterns

## Required review gates

- Writing quality review
- Enterprise architecture review
- Microsoft technical review
- TOGAF / architecture method review
- Policy / governance review
- Final human editorial review

## Current working assumption

This document is a working draft. It is not an approved HC/PHAC policy instrument until formally reviewed and approved through the appropriate governance path.
